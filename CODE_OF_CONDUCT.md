# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Brok-AI is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in our projects and community spaces.

We value constructive collaboration, empathy, and thoughtful technical discussion.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Treating people with respect and consideration.
- Communicating professionally and constructively.
- Welcoming different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Giving and receiving feedback respectfully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and work, not personal characteristics.
- Respecting privacy and consent.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or threats.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate humiliation.
- Trolling, abusive language, or persistent disruptive conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Unwanted attention or stalking.
- Deliberately disrupting project or community activities.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Brok-AI organization repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing Brok-AI.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code, contact the organization maintainers privately through GitHub. Include relevant context and evidence where possible. Do not use public issues to disclose sensitive personal information.

Reports will be reviewed as responsibly and confidentially as circumstances allow.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take appropriate action based on the circumstances and severity of a violation. Actions may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions, or removal from organization-managed spaces.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in a review is not acceptable.
